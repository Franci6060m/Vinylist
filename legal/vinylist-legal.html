Part One

Privacy Policy

How Vinylist Music Player handles information on your device — sections P1 through P10.

Short version: Vinylist has no account, no login, no analytics, and no ad network — Francis&M_Tech never sees your music library, lyrics, playlists, or listening stats. A couple of optional features you have to actively choose to use — lyrics search and update checking — send a small, specific piece of information to outside services to work. P6 lists exactly what.

P2Who we are

Vinylist Music Player is developed and published by Francis&M_Tech, an independent software studio. This policy applies to the Vinylist Android app and its offline web build, wherever installed.

P3Data we don't collect

Vinylist has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server of its own. Francis&M_Tech never receives, sees, or stores your identity information, the contents of your library, your lyrics, playlists, ratings, listening history, location, or contacts.

There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service silently phoning home in the background. The only network requests the app makes are the ones you trigger yourself — see P6.

P4What's stored on your device

To function as a music player, Vinylist saves the following locally on your device only (IndexedDB and local storage) — never on a remote server:

  • Your music library — references to (or on-device copies of) audio files you add, including tracks linked directly from your phone's device library without copying the file
  • Playlists you create, and favorite/like/dislike ratings
  • Lyrics you paste in or find with lyrics search, including any Tap Sync timing you record
  • Listening statistics used to build your weekly Report
  • App preferences — theme, skin (Glassmorphism, Vintage Tape Deck, or Neumorphism), visualizer style, equalizer curve, playback speed, and similar settings

Because all of this lives only on your device, Francis&M_Tech has no way to access, recover, or view it — including if you contact us for support. You remain solely responsible for this content, as set out in Part Two.

P5Permissions we request

The Android app requests the following device permissions. None are used to collect or transmit data about you — each exists solely to make a specific feature work:

PermissionWhy Vinylist asks for it
Audio / storage readLets you pick songs and folders, or link tracks from your device's media library, to add to Vinylist. Files are read to play them — nothing is uploaded.
Storage write (Android 12 and below)Only used if you tap "Save a copy to phone" for a track's lyrics — writes that one file to Documents/Music Lyrics. Not needed on Android 13+.
Foreground serviceKeeps playback and lock-screen controls running reliably while you use other apps.
Wake lockPrevents playback from being interrupted when the screen turns off.
NotificationsUsed only for local alerts you trigger yourself, such as "your weekly report is ready." Never used for marketing.
InternetOnly used when you tap Search lyrics online, or Check for updates. See P6.
Install unknown appsOnly requested if you download an in-app update and tap Install.
Unrestricted battery useOptional, off by default. Stops your phone's battery manager from pausing background playback. Never used to collect data.

You can review or revoke any of these at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Vinylist → Permissions (battery use is under Settings → Apps → Vinylist → Battery).

P6Third-party resources

Vinylist loads its typefaces and a small number of open-source code libraries from public content-delivery networks on first install, then runs fully offline. Two features make their own network requests, only when you start them: lyrics search sends the song title and artist you enter to lrclib.net, lyrics.ovh, and textyl.co, and shows you what they return; update checking asks GitHub's public release API for the latest Vinylist release, and downloads the file directly from GitHub if you choose to update. No personal data is included in either request, and none of these services receive your library, lyrics, or listening activity.

P7Children's privacy

Vinylist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age. If you believe a child has provided us information through the contact channel below, let us know and we'll delete it.

P8Retention & deletion

Your data is retained on-device for as long as you keep the app installed. To remove everything Vinylist has ever stored, uninstall the app or clear its storage/data from your device's App info screen. Either action permanently and immediately deletes your library, lyrics, ratings, stats, and settings, since no copy exists anywhere else.

P9Security

Because your data never leaves your device, the main safeguard is your device's own lock screen and storage encryption. We recommend keeping your phone's OS updated and using a screen lock, as you would for any app storing personal media.

P10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Vinylist evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Vinylist after an update means you accept the revised policy.
