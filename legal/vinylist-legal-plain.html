Part One: Privacy Policy

How Vinylist Music Player handles information on your device.

1. Short version

Your local music library, lyrics, playlists, ratings, and listening stats need no account, stay on your device, and are never collected, transmitted, or sold. Signing up for a free Vinylist account is entirely optional and only powers the Online tab — a shared space for discovering and uploading tracks/playlists — which is the one part of the app that involves a server. A couple of other optional features you have to actively choose to use — lyrics search and update checking — send a small, specific piece of information to outside services to work. Sections 6 and 7 list exactly what.

2. Who we are

Vinylist Music Player is developed and published by Francis&M_Tech, an independent software studio. This policy applies to the Vinylist Android app and its offline web build, wherever installed.

3. Data we don't collect

Your local music library, playlists, lyrics, ratings, and listening stats need no account and are never collected, transmitted, or sold. Unless you choose to create a Vinylist account (section 5), Francis&M_Tech never receives, sees, or stores:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any identity information
  • The contents of your music library, or the audio files themselves
  • Your lyrics, playlists, ratings, or listening history
  • Location, contacts, or any other device data unrelated to playing audio

There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service silently phoning home in the background. Aside from the optional account described in section 5, the only network requests the app makes are the ones you trigger yourself — see section 7.

4. What's stored on your device

To function as a music player, Vinylist saves the following locally on your device only, using your browser/OS storage (IndexedDB and local storage) — never on a remote server:

  • Your music library — references to (or on-device copies of) audio files you add, including tracks linked directly from your phone's device library without copying the file
  • Playlists you create, and favorite/like/dislike ratings
  • Lyrics you paste in or find with lyrics search, including any Tap Sync timing you record
  • Listening statistics used to build your weekly Report
  • App preferences — theme, skin (Glassmorphism, Vintage Tape Deck, or Neumorphism), visualizer style, equalizer curve, playback speed, and similar settings

Because all of this lives only on your device, Francis&M_Tech has no way to access, recover, or view it — including if you contact us for support. You remain solely responsible for this content, as set out in Part Two.

5. Accounts and the Online tab

Vinylist is first and foremost a personal, offline-first music player — your local library, playlists, lyrics, and stats work entirely on-device with no account required. Vinylist also includes the Online tab: an optional, shared space where you can create a free account to discover, upload, and share tracks or playlists with other Vinylist users. Creating an account is entirely your choice and only needed to upload to, or follow people on, the Online tab — your local music and listening stats never touch it.

If you create a Vinylist account, your email, username, display name, and bio are stored on Vinylist's backend so your profile can appear on the Online tab. Anything you upload there — track/playlist titles, descriptions, cover art, and the audio files themselves — is stored on that backend too. Who you follow is stored the same way. None of this touches your local library or listening stats, which stay device-only regardless of whether you have an account. You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for anything posted, uploaded, or shared under your account.

6. Permissions we request

The Android app requests the following device permissions. None are used to collect or transmit data about you — each exists solely to make a specific feature work:

PermissionWhy Vinylist asks for it
Audio / storage readLets you pick songs and folders, or link tracks from your device's media library, to add to Vinylist. Files are read to play them — nothing is uploaded.
Storage write (Android 12 and below)Only used if you tap "Save a copy to phone" for a track's lyrics — writes that one file to Documents/Music Lyrics. Not needed on Android 13+.
Foreground serviceKeeps playback and lock-screen controls running reliably while you use other apps.
Wake lockPrevents playback from being interrupted when the screen turns off.
NotificationsUsed only for local alerts you trigger yourself, such as "your weekly report is ready." Never used for marketing.
InternetUsed when you tap Search lyrics online or Check for updates, and for the optional Online tab (signing in, uploading, browsing the shared feed). See sections 5 and 7.
Install unknown appsOnly requested if you download an in-app update and tap Install.
Unrestricted battery useOptional, off by default. Stops your phone's battery manager from pausing background playback. Never used to collect data.

You can review or revoke any of these at any time in your device's Settings, under Apps, then Vinylist, then Permissions (battery use is under Settings, Apps, Vinylist, Battery).

7. Third-party resources

Vinylist loads its typefaces and a small number of open-source code libraries (used for its interface and icons) from public content-delivery networks the first time it's installed, after which they're cached and the app runs fully offline.

A few features make their own network requests, only when you deliberately start them or opt in:

  • Lyrics search — sends the song title and artist you enter to three independent lyrics lookup services (lrclib.net, lyrics.ovh, and textyl.co) and shows you whatever they return. Nothing else about you or your library is sent.
  • App updates — checking for updates queries GitHub's public release API (api.github.com); downloading one fetches the file directly from GitHub. No personal data is included in either request.
  • The Online tab — accounts, the shared feed, and uploads run on Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider (including file storage for uploaded audio/cover art). Nothing is shared with Supabase unless you create an account or use the Online tab.

None of these services are operated by Francis&M_Tech, and none of them receive your local library, lyrics, or listening activity beyond the minimal data described above.

8. Children's privacy

Vinylist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age, including through account sign-up. If you believe a child has provided us information through the contact details below or created an account, let us know and we'll delete it.

9. Retention and deletion

On-device data (your library, lyrics, ratings, stats, and settings) is retained for as long as you keep the app installed. To remove it, uninstall the app, or clear its storage and data from your device's App info screen. Either action permanently and immediately deletes everything stored on-device, since no copy exists anywhere else. If you have a Vinylist account, anything you've uploaded to the Online tab and your profile stay on Vinylist's backend until you delete them or ask Francis&M_Tech to via the contact details below.

10. Security

Your on-device data is protected mainly by your device's own lock screen and storage encryption — we recommend keeping your phone's OS updated and using a screen lock. If you create a Vinylist account, its credentials and backend data are protected by Supabase's infrastructure; use a unique password and keep your login secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Vinylist evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this document. Continuing to use Vinylist after an update means you accept the revised policy.
