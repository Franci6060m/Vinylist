Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vinylist Music Player, built and maintained by FrancisTech, handles information on your device. In short: Vinylist keeps everything local — there is no account, no server, and nothing to leak.

Short version: Vinylist doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. There's no login, no analytics, and no ad network. Your music library, playlists, lyrics, ratings, and listening stats never leave your phone.

01Who we are

Vinylist Music Player is developed and published by FrancisTech (Francis Tech Ltd), an independent software studio. This policy applies to the Vinylist Android app and its offline web build, wherever installed.

02Data we don't collect

Vinylist has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server of its own. As a direct result, FrancisTech never receives, sees, or stores:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any identity information
  • The contents of your music library, or the audio files themselves
  • Your lyrics, playlists, ratings, or listening history
  • Location, contacts, or any other device data unrelated to playing audio

There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service silently phoning home in the background.

03What's stored on your device

To function as a music player, Vinylist saves the following locally on your device only, using your browser/OS storage (IndexedDB and local storage) — never on a remote server:

  • Your music library — references to (or on-device copies of) audio files you add
  • Playlists you create, and favorite/like/dislike ratings
  • Lyrics you paste in, including any Tap Sync timing you record
  • Listening statistics used to build your weekly Report
  • App preferences — theme, skin, equalizer curve, playback speed, and similar settings

Because all of this lives only on your device, FrancisTech has no way to access, recover, or view it — including if you contact us for support.

04Permissions we request

The Android app requests a small set of device permissions, each used strictly for the purpose below and never to collect or transmit data:

PermissionWhy Vinylist asks for it
Audio / storage readLets you pick songs and folders from your device to add to your library. Files are read to play them — nothing is uploaded.
Foreground serviceKeeps playback and lock-screen controls running reliably while you use other apps.
Wake lockPrevents playback from being interrupted when the screen turns off.
NotificationsUsed only for local alerts you trigger yourself, such as "your weekly report is ready." Never used for marketing.

You can review or revoke any of these at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Vinylist → Permissions.

05Third-party resources

Vinylist loads its typefaces and a small number of open-source code libraries (used for its interface and icons) from public content-delivery networks the first time it's installed, after which they're cached and the app runs fully offline. These are static files — no library used by Vinylist reports your activity back to its provider through the app.

06Children's privacy

Vinylist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — which follows naturally from collecting no information at all. If you believe a child has provided us information through the Contact channel below, let us know and we'll delete it.

07Retention & deletion

Your data is retained on-device for as long as you keep the app installed. To remove everything Vinylist has ever stored:

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Clear the app's storage/data from your device's App info screen

Either action permanently and immediately deletes your library, lyrics, ratings, stats, and settings, since no copy exists anywhere else.

08Security

Because your data never leaves your device, the main safeguard is your device's own lock screen and storage encryption. We recommend keeping your phone's OS updated and using a screen lock, as you would for any app storing personal media.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Vinylist evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use Vinylist after an update means you accept the revised policy.
